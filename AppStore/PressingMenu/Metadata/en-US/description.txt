Pressing Menu puts Pressing in your Mac's menu bar. Pressing is the iPhone app that streams the record on your turntable to your Sonos Rooms. Pressing Menu shows what it's playing and changes where it plays, so you don't have to get up and go to the phone.

WHAT'S PLAYING, AT A GLANCE
Click the record in the menu bar. The panel shows the current Track's title large, with the artist beneath it, and the last 10 Tracks below, newest first. Until a Track is identified, it says what Pressing is doing: waiting for a Record, or playing one that isn't identified yet.

A NOTIFICATION FOR EACH NEW TRACK
When Pressing identifies a new Track, Pressing Menu shows a notification with its title, artist and cover. Click it to open the panel. Switch notifications off in Settings.

YOUR ROOMS, FROM THE MAC
Click the summary in the panel's footer, such as "Living Room +1", to see the Rooms. Tick a Sonos Room to switch it on at once, and drag its slider to set its volume. Switch "This iPhone" on or off, and see where it plays. A change made on the iPhone or in the Sonos app shows on the Mac straight away.

FIND A SONG
Right-click a Track to search for it in Spotify or Apple Music, whichever you choose in Settings.

AT HOME IN THE MENU BAR
There's no Dock icon and nothing to type in. Pressing Menu finds Pressing on your network by itself, and reconnects when the phone comes back. The menu-bar icon shows when a Record is playing and when the iPhone can't be reached. Choose light, dark or System appearance, and launch at login.

THE IPHONE STAYS IN CHARGE
Pressing Menu only shows Pressing and sends the same changes as the phone's own controls. Quitting it leaves the music playing.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Pressing, open on an iPhone on the same network as the Mac
• A Mac with macOS 26 or later

Pressing Menu talks only to Pressing on your home network. It has no account or server of its own.

## Description

2103 of 4,000 characters.

```text
First Press Menu puts First Press in your Mac's menu bar. First Press is the iPhone app that streams the record on your turntable to your Sonos Rooms. First Press Menu shows what it's playing and changes where it plays, so you don't have to get up and go to the phone.

WHAT'S PLAYING, AT A GLANCE
Click the record in the menu bar. The panel shows the current Track's cover and title, with the artist and album beneath, and the last 10 Tracks below, newest first. Until a Track is identified, it says what First Press is doing: waiting for a Record, playing one that isn't identified yet, or between sides.

A NOTIFICATION FOR EACH NEW TRACK
When First Press identifies a new Track, First Press Menu shows a notification with its title, artist and cover. When a side runs out, a notification tells you to flip the record. Click one to open the panel. Switch notifications off in Settings.

YOUR ROOMS, FROM THE MAC
Click the summary in the panel's footer, such as "Living Room +1", to see the Rooms. Tick a Sonos Room to switch it on at once, and drag its slider to set its volume. Switch "This iPhone" on or off, and see where it plays. A change made on the iPhone or in the Sonos app shows on the Mac straight away.

FIND A SONG
Click a Track to search for it in Spotify or Apple Music, whichever you choose in Settings.

AT HOME IN THE MENU BAR
There's no Dock icon and nothing to type in. First Press Menu finds First Press on your network by itself, and reconnects when the phone comes back. The menu-bar icon shows when a Record is playing and when the iPhone can't be reached. Choose light, dark or System appearance, and launch at login.

THE IPHONE STAYS IN CHARGE
First Press Menu only shows First Press and sends the same changes as the phone's own controls. Quitting it leaves the music playing.

WHAT YOU NEED
• First Press, open on an iPhone on the same network as the Mac
• A Mac with macOS 26 or later

First Press Menu talks only to First Press on your home network. It has no account or server of its own.

First Press Menu isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Sonos, Apple or Spotify.
```

First Press turns a spare iPhone into a companion for your record deck. Put a record on, and First Press plays it in the Sonos Rooms you've chosen, shows what's playing and keeps a History. You don't have to touch the phone.

STARTS BY ITSELF
Drop the needle and First Press starts. When the side runs out, it waits while you flip the record. If nothing new starts, it stops the speakers after 45 seconds of silence, even with the stylus still in the run-out groove.

PLAYS IN YOUR SONOS ROOMS
Tick any number of Rooms, each at its own volume, and they play in sync. If someone takes a Room back in the Sonos app, First Press steps back and leaves it alone until the next record.

PLAYS ON THE PHONE TOO
"This iPhone" plays the record on the phone's speaker, Bluetooth earphones or an AirPlay receiver as well.

KNOWS WHAT'S PLAYING
Each Track is identified with Shazam. Now Playing shows the cover, title, artist and album large enough to read across the room, and the Sonos app shows the artist and title too.

KEEPS A HISTORY
The Tracks you've played sit under Now Playing, newest first. Tap one to look it up on Spotify.

SCROBBLES TO LAST.FM
Connect your Last.fm account and every Track that qualifies is scrobbled. Scrobbles wait while you're offline and are sent when the network returns.

BEHAVES LIKE AN APPLIANCE
The screen never blanks while a record plays, and blanks after a quiet spell to protect the display. Kiosk mode hides the status bar and the controls until you touch the screen.

WHAT YOU NEED
• A turntable with USB audio: a deck with a USB output, or a phono preamp feeding a USB audio interface
• A camera adapter with a charging port, so the phone charges while the deck is plugged in
• Sonos speakers on the same Wi-Fi network
• Optional: a Last.fm account

First Press only listens to the input you choose. It controls your speakers over your home network and has no account or server of its own.
